# Changelog

All notable changes to Eglantine are documented here. Each release's notes are taken from its
`## [X.Y.Z]` section (release candidates use the section of the version they are a candidate for).
New entries go under `## [Unreleased]`; `tools/ci/bump-version.sh` turns them into a version section.

## [Unreleased]

- HDR output (Vulkan): Graphics > "HDR output" presents in HDR10 (or scRGB) when the display has
  HDR on, and tells Switch 2 games their display shows HDR so they output HDR10. Peak brightness
  and paper white are configurable; SDR games and menus are shown at paper white.
